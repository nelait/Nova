FAQ and troubleshooting

Adoption

Do we have to replace our recordkeeper? No. The recordkeeper stays the system of record. The hub orchestrates the work around it and commits results through a configured integration.

Can we change a process without a developer? Process steps, rules, email templates, client variations and AI agents are all configuration files under config/. Business analysts can change them; review changes like code and run npm test, which runs every rule set's own test cases.

Does it work without AI? Yes. Claude is optional. Without it, Ask Claude is hidden and AI agent steps ask the person to enter the result by hand.

Can AI change our records or skip a step? No. AI never decides which steps run. Its output is a proposal a person approves, agents can only use read-only lookups, and the hub blocks any email that drops a required fact.

How do clients with special service terms fit in? Each client can have a variant of any process (extra steps, different automation, its own templates or rule sets) without forking the standard process. See Process definitions.

Can we keep one client's plans away from staff who don't serve that client? Yes. Give people client:<clientId> groups for the clients they serve; they then see and are routed only those clients' work. Supervisors and admins see all clients. See Client access.

How do people sign in? Through your identity provider, with OpenID Connect (Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace, Ping, Auth0) or SAML 2.0 (ADFS, Shibboleth, or SAML apps in those providers). Plan Ops Hub never handles passwords, and people must also be active users in its directory. See Sign-in.

Can our internal audit or compliance reviewers look without changing anything? Yes. Give them the role:auditor group. (Other roles split up administration: supervisor, user-admin and integration-admin; you can define your own in config/roles.json. See Roles and permissions.) Auditors see every case, its audit trail, emails and integration calls, and can filter and download the full audit log as CSV. They can't change anything and are never assigned work. See Roles and permissions.

Troubleshooting

"You don't have access yet" after signing in. The person signed in at the identity provider but isn't an active Plan Ops Hub user with that email. Import them through SCIM or reactivate them.

The server won't start and names a playbook, rule set, integration or agent. Configuration is checked at startup. The message names the file, the step or rule, and the problem, for example Unknown name "nope" or uses unknown rule set.

A task says "exception". An automatic step failed. The message says why: a failed rule, an unreachable system after retries, or an AI agent error. Fix the cause and click Retry, or Override with a reason.

A task is in the unassigned queue. No active user holds the skill at the required level with spare capacity. Add or cross-train someone (SCIM import), raise a capacity, or reassign work. Routing runs again automatically.

An email can't be sent: "missing required facts". The edited email dropped a value the template inserted, such as a fee or date. Put the value back. It may be reformatted ($2,500 for 2500) but not removed.

"secret TRAC_API_KEY is not set". A real recordkeeper URL is configured without its key. Set the environment variable, or unset TRAC_BASE_URL to use the mock.

Ask Claude or an agent says AI is not configured. Set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and restart.
